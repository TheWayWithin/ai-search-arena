## What AI Search Arena Does

Every month, AI Search Arena will:

1. **Define standardized prompts** across 51 scoring dimensions — from citation accuracy and schema markup to pricing transparency and onboarding experience
2. **Send those prompts to 6 different AI models** (GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, Command R+, Mistral Large, Llama 3.1) via OpenRouter
3. **Synthesize scores using median aggregation** — no single model can skew the results
4. **Derive confidence tags** from inter-model agreement — you'll know when models agree (High confidence) and when they don't (Low)
5. **Calculate weighted composite scores** with dense ranking — tied scores share the same rank, and dimensions that don't apply get their weights redistributed fairly
6. **Publish everything** — the methodology, the scores, the evidence, even audit packages with SHA-256 integrity hashes

Vendors get a 5 business day review window to submit factual corrections before publication. Transparency isn't optional — it's the entire point.
