> ## Killed: AI Search Arena, September 2026
>
> This was a monthly independent benchmark of AI search optimisation tools.
> Thirty-two tools were scored by six models across 51 dimensions, with the
> methodology open at [geo-benchmark-framework](https://github.com/TheWayWithin/geo-benchmark-framework)
> under CC BY 4.0. Phases one to four were built on 1 March 2026 and the first
> cycle ran on 3 March: 9,792 evaluations, 1,632 synthesised scores. Two cycles
> were published. The last product change was the canonical URL fix of 11 May 2026.
>
> I decided to kill it on 13 September 2026. The site had been returning a server
> error since at least 12 September, the monthly cadence had broken after two
> cycles, and I chose not to fix it. The Vercel project was deleted on
> 13 September 2026 and the domain aisearcharena.com is being allowed to lapse.
> The Neon database is to be deleted separately.
>
> The repo stays public and read-only as the record of what was built. The
> methodology repo above is unaffected and remains open.
